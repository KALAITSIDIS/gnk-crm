## Shell

- Sidebar (`<nav aria-label="Main">`, `aria-current` on the current module): Dashboard, Leads, Pipeline, Deals, Properties, Contacts, Viewings, Tasks, Keys, Share links, Reports, Calculators, Settings(A). Collapsible; icons from lucide.
- Topbar: global fuzzy search (properties+contacts, ⌘K; below sm an icon-only Search button opens the same dialog), quick-add menu (Property / Contact / Lead / Viewing), user menu.
- Mobile: bottom nav with Dashboard, Leads, Pipeline, Viewings, More.
- Every screen's tab title is "<screen> · GN REOS"; a property by its reference; contact, deal and viewing records by a generic word ("Contact", "Deal", "Viewing"), never a name. The buyer page `/p/[token]` reads "Property information".
- Focus: one visible outline on every link, button and control (white on the navy sidebar and drawer). A status, banner or error opens with a plain sentence; codes and technical names sit under a "Technical details" disclosure (doc 06, Tone).

## Dashboard contents

**Admin:** alert banners (cron health) · Listing health (the worklist's fresh computation: website essentials, completeness, longest on the market / price review, data warnings; "unreadable" on a failed read) · **My day** (the admin's own day: today's viewings · tasks due today & overdue, labelled by kind, each opening its record · my open leads, unanswered first, with clocks · hot buyers with no logged contact — a call, a logged conversation or "contacted" — on them, their leads or deals for ≥3 days; a chat-link click is not contact) · **Team** (unassigned open leads · overdue across the team, unassigned tasks included) · KPI row (open pipeline €, won this month €, new leads 7d, first-response mean/median/p90 7d) · pipeline-by-stage bar · leads-by-source 30d · **Inventory by status** (per live status a total of records of every kind → `/properties?status=S&kind=all`; per-kind chips → `?status=S&kind=K`; "Saleable available: N — X listings + Y units", containers excluded; a footnote defining live records; "Withdrawn & archived →" `/properties?scope=archived&kind=all`; from `property_inventory_counts()`, 0153) · mandates expiring ≤30d · latest sales activity (10) · settings & admin changes (5).
**Agent 📱:** quick actions · My day (the same cards; each task opens its record, each lead its inbox or its person).
Fixed per role (guardrail 6): no personal/team toggle.

## Empty/error states

Every list has: empty state with primary action, loading skeletons, error boundary with retry. Forms: zod messages inline; server errors as toast + field mapping.
